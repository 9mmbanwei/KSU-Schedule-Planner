About the data. Sections, times, rooms, instructors and seat counts come from KSU's class search at the time shown above, and seats change constantly during registration. Major requirements come from KSU's 2025–26 curriculum sheets (Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology) and the 2025 Architecture curriculum chart, entered by hand: prerequisites are simplified, rules like "at least one course from List 1" aren't enforced, and the official catalog and DegreeWorks always win. Mark courses Done to match your DegreeWorks audit. Professor names link to a Rate My Professors search; use Prefer and Avoid to steer the schedules. This is an unofficial student tool, not affiliated with Kennesaw State University. Always confirm in Owl Express before you register.
